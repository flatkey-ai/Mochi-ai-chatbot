# Code of Conduct

Mochi is a friendly project, and we want everyone who takes part to feel welcome.

**Please do**
- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

**Please don't**
- Harass, insult or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Post sexual content, slurs or personal attacks.
- Share other people's private information without their permission.

**Reporting.** If something makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through the [Flatkey contact form](https://flatkey.ai/contact?utm_source=mochi_bot&utm_medium=github&utm_campaign=mochi&utm_content=code_of_conduct). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or block people who don't follow this code.
